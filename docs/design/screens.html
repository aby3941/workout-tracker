The rules

  • Main lifts: hit all your 5s → next time add 2.5 kg (deadlift 5 kg).
  • Accessories: every set at the top of the range → add weight. Under the bottom → go lighter.
  • 3 misses in a row at the same weight → take 10% off and build back up.
  • Starting weight: work up in sets of 5 until the bar slows, then take 2.5 kg off.
